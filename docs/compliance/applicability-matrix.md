# Regulatory applicability matrix

**Status: UNAPPROVED — DO NOT USE LIVE DATA**

This matrix maps each Clinic Ops feature to the instruments a qualified
reviewer has to read before that feature touches real data. It's not legal
advice and it doesn't decide applicability. Every role, basis, retention
period and notice below is a **candidate** for review under the external
gate named in the Approvals column. No gate is satisfied by this file, and
nothing here grants authority to use live data. See
[LIVE-DATA-GATE.md](LIVE-DATA-GATE.md).

Source: the RG table of the executable plan, expanded to one row per feature
and checked against primary texts on the retrieved date. Gate ids follow
[the successor contract](../plans/clinic-ops-premium-successor.md#external-gates).
Per-capability AI records live in [ai-risk-register.md](ai-risk-register.md);
open checks live in [reverification-log.md](reverification-log.md).

## How to read the columns

- **Controller/operator role**: the LGPD agent-of-treatment split Clinic Ops
  plans for. "Clinic" means the customer clinic, "Clinic Ops" the SaaS
  vendor, "provider" an external processor (ASR, LLM, video, messaging, PSP).
  For AI rows the CFM 2.454/2026 roles (developer, distributor, contracting
  institution, Anexo I items V, IX and XII) are listed too.
- **Basis candidate**: the LGPD hypothesis a reviewer should test first.
  Articles are cited, not concluded.
- **Approvals**: the external gates (EG-n) whose records must exist before the
  feature is used with real data. Listing a gate here doesn't mean it's open,
  scheduled or cleared.
- **Source URL**: the primary texts fetched for this row. Several URLs are
  separated by `;`.
- **Retrieved date**: the date of the last retrieval attempt for the row's
  sources (ISO 8601).
- **Verification level**:
  - `verified`: every primary text the row relies on was fetched from its
    official publisher on the retrieved date, and the facts recorded in the
    row appear in that text. It says nothing about whether the instrument
    applies; the reviewer decides that.
  - `search-snippet`: at least one fact comes from a secondary summary, a
    search result or earlier plan research, and its primary text wasn't
    fetched (or didn't contain the fact) in this pass.
  - `unverified`: at least one instrument the row relies on couldn't be
    retrieved.

  A row takes the weakest level among its sources. Every row below
  `verified` has an open step in [reverification-log.md](reverification-log.md).

## Facts recorded from the retrieved texts

These are paraphrased facts, not interpretations. The page and HTTP details
are in [reverification-log.md](reverification-log.md).

- CFM Res. 2.454/2026 was approved on 11/02/2026, published in the DOU on
  27/02/2026 (rectified 05/03/2026), and art. 23 sets entry into force 180
  days after publication. Art. 13 names four risk levels (low, medium, high,
  unacceptable). Anexo II, as retrieved, defines low, medium and high; no
  definition of "unacceptable" was found in the retrieved text. Art. 12 puts
  the preliminary risk assessment on institutions that develop or use AI.
  Art. 14 sole paragraph requires an AI and Telemedicine Commission in health
  institutions that adopt their own AI systems.
- RDC 657/2022 art. 1 §2 III excludes software used only for administrative
  and financial management in health services from its scope. Its SaMD
  definition includes subscription software hosted centrally (SaaS).
- RDC 751/2022 Regra 11 classifies software that provides information for
  diagnostic or therapeutic decisions as class II, rising to class III or IV
  with the severity of the possible impact; other SaMD is class I.
- RDC 873/2024 institutes SNCR for numbering prescription notifications and
  prescription pads; art. 16 made it mandatory from 1 July 2025. The Anvisa
  RDC page lists later amendments (RDC 1.000/2025), which weren't read.
- CD/ANPD Res. 19/2024 (published 23/08/2024) gives agents that use
  contractual clauses up to 12 months from publication to incorporate the
  ANPD standard clauses.
- CD/ANPD Res. 15/2024 (published 26/04/2024) sets three working days for
  incident communication to ANPD (art. 6) and to data subjects (art. 9).
